Pictures for the tiles. Included: ps5, bein, matches, youtube, disney, shahid (logos belong to their companies).

Put a PNG here, then run install.bat again:
  ps5.png      -> the PS5 tile
  bein.png     -> the beIN tile
  matches.png  -> the Matches tile
  youtube.png, netflix.png, shahid.png, disney.png, appletv.png, plex.png, ...  -> that app's tile

Best: a logo on a transparent background, at least 256 pixels wide.
Plume turns it into a single-colour mark (Mono) or keeps its colour (Colour), like the other apps.
Without a file, the tile shows the TV's own icon for the app, a symbol, or its name.
